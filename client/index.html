What is Karthificial ?

Karthificial is an educational website created for Tamil Nadu State Board 10th and 12th standard students. It provides organized notes, previous question papers, subject resources, and career guidance in one clean learning platform.

📘

Curated Notes

Well-arranged notes for important subjects, made simple for quick revision and exam preparation.

📄

Past Question Papers

Access previous year question papers to understand exam patterns, repeated questions, and important topics.

🎯

Career Guidance

Helpful guidance for students to choose courses, careers, and next steps after school.
