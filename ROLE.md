## Team Overview

Karthificial Website was developed by a focused three-member team. The project combined frontend development, admin-managed content, authentication, database integration, media upload handling, MCQ practice, analytics, documentation, deployment, and continuous visual testing.

The team worked collaboratively throughout the development phase. Each member handled a different part of the platform while also supporting debugging, testing, and final polishing. The result is an education-focused web platform built for Tamil Nadu State Board students, especially 10th and 12th standard learners.
