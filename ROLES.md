## Team Overview

Karthificial Website was developed by a focused team of three members, with each member contributing to different areas of the project including frontend development, backend development, database integration, authentication, analytics, SEO, UI/UX design, debugging, deployment, and documentation.

The team worked consistently for more than 8 hours every day during the active development phase to improve the quality, stability, design, and overall functionality of the platform. The project followed a collaborative workflow where frontend, backend, design, and deployment tasks were handled together to build a complete education-focused web application.
